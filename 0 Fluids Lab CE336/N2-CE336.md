## energy loss

![[Pasted image 20250904104017.png]]

![[Pasted image 20250904104026.png]]
head loss due to friction: (Darcy-Weisback equation)
$$h_{f}=f \frac{L}{D} \frac{v^2}{2g}$$
friction factor for laminar flow
$$f=\frac{64\mu}{\rho VD}=\frac{64}{Re}$$
$$Re=\frac{VD}{\nu};\quad \nu=\frac{\mu}{\rho};\quad \tau=\frac{\mu dv}{dy}$$

![[Pasted image 20250904104044.png]]
laminar flow: $Re<2000$
turbulent flow: $Re>2000$
$e$ - average roughness of pipe wall
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{ f }}=2\log\left( 3.7 \frac{D}{e} \right)$$

## frictionless flow in Venturi meter

in a venturi contraction, pressure drops and flow increases

$$Q_{1}=Q_{2}$$
$$A_{1}V_{1}=A_{2}V_{2}$$

$$\frac{p_{1}}{\gamma}+\frac{v_{1}^2}{2g}=\frac{p_{2}}{\gamma}+\frac{v_{2}^2}{2g}$$
$$\frac{p_{1}-p_{2}}{\gamma}=\frac{v_{2}^2-v_{1}^2}{2g}$$
