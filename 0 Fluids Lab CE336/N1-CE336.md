What is a fluid?
a substance that deforms continuously when acted on by a shearing stress of any size

$P=\frac{F}{A}$

in static fluids, fluid is at rest and force is normal
dynamic fluids induce shear stress 

$P=\gamma h$

$P_{atm}$ = 10.3m of water

$P_{abs}=P_{atm}+P_{gage}$
