Ideal gas law:
$$pV=nR_{u}T$$
$p=$ absolute pressure
$V=$volume
$T=$ absolute temperature
$n=$ number of moles
$$1gmol=6.22\times 10^{23}\text{particles}$$
$$R_{u}=8.314$$
$$R_{u}=MR$$
$M=$ molecular weight
$R=$ engineering gas constant

equation of state:
$$\rho=\frac{P}{RT}$$
Sample 1
![[Pasted image 20250828142141.png]]
$$R=MR_{u}=\left( 70.9 \frac{slug}{mol} \right)(8.314)=701.1 \frac{ftlb}{slug^{\circ} R}$$
$$T=80+459.7=539.7^{\circ}  R$$
$$\rho=\frac{p}{RT}=\frac{100psi\left( 144 \frac{psf}{psi} \right)}{\left( 701.1 \frac{ftlb}{slug ^{\circ}  R} \right)(539.7^{\circ} R)}=.0381 \frac{slug}{ft^3}$$
$$\gamma=\rho g=\left( .0381 \frac{slug}{ft^3} \right)\left( 32.2 \frac{ft}{s^2} \right)=1.23 \frac{lb}{ft^3}$$
$$\alpha=\frac{1}{\rho}=\frac{1}{.0381}=26.3 \frac{ft^3}{slug}$$

Compressibility of Fluids
bulk modulus of elasticity:
$$E=- \frac{dp}{\frac{dV}{V}}$$

Isothermal (constant temp):
$$\frac{p}{\gamma}=\frac{p}{\rho}=\text{constant};\quad E=p$$

Isentropic (no heat exchange):
$$\frac{p}{\gamma^k}=\frac{p}{\rho^k}=\text{constant};\quad E=kp$$

Ex: Helium Compression
![[Pasted image 20250828143558.png]]
Isothermal:
$$\frac{p_{1}}{\rho_{1}}=\frac{p_{2}}{\rho_{2}}\to p_{2}=\frac{\rho_{2}p_{1}}{\rho_{1}}=\frac{m_{2}V_{1}p_{1}}{m_{1}V_{2}}=\frac{V_{1}p_{1}}{V_{2}}=\frac{p_{1}}{.5ft^3}=29.4psia$$
$$E=p=29.4psia$$
$$T_{2}=T_{1}=80^{\circ} F$$

Isentropic:
$$\frac{p_{1}}{\rho_{1}^k}=\frac{p_{2}}{\rho_{2}^k}\to p_{2}=\left( \frac{\rho_{2}}{\rho_{1}} \right)^kp_{1}=(2)^{1.66}(14.7psia)=46.5psia$$
$$E=kp=77.1psia$$
$$\frac{\rho_{1}T_{1}}{p_{1}}=\frac{\rho_{2}T_{2}}{p_{2}}\to T_{2}=\frac{\rho_{1}T_{1}p_{2}}{p_{1}\rho_{2}}=\frac{\rho_{1}}{\rho_{2}} \frac{p_{2}}{p_{1}}T_{1}$$
$$T_{2}=(2)\left( \frac{46.5psia}{14.7psia} \right)(539.7)=853.6^{\circ} R$$
