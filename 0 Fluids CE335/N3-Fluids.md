$$\frac{p_{1}}{\gamma}+z_{1}+\frac{v_{1}^2}{2g}+E_{p}=\frac{p_{2}}{\gamma}+z_{2}+\frac{v_{2}^2}{2g}+E_{t}$$
Power is head time weight flow rate
$$P=H\cdot \gamma \cdot Q$$

![[Pasted image 20251014145945.png]]


![[Pasted image 20251014145954.png]]

$$V_{2}=\frac{Q}{A}=\frac{19 \frac{m^3}{s}}{\frac{\pi}{4}2^2}=\frac{19}{\pi}=6.05 \frac{m}{s}$$
$$E_{T}=h-\frac{V_{2}^2}{2g}=29m-\frac{\left( 6.05 \frac{m}{s} \right)^2}{2(9.81)}=27.14m$$
