ex Mixing Fresh and Ocean Water

$$\gamma_{o}=10.15 \frac{kN}{m^3};\quad \gamma_{f}=\frac{9.7kN}{m^3}$$
$$Q_{o}=.08 \frac{m^3}{s};\quad Q_{f}=.10 \frac{m^3}{s}$$
$$Q_{b}=Q_{o}+Q_{f}=.28 \frac{m^3}{s}$$
$$\dot{m}_{o}+\dot{m}_{f}=\dot{m}_{f}$$
$$\rho Q_{o}+\rho Q_{f}=\rho Q_{b}$$
$$\frac{\gamma Q_{o}}{g}+\frac{\gamma Q_{f}}{g}=\frac{\gamma Q_{b}}{g}$$
$$\rho_{b}=\frac{\gamma Q_{o}+\gamma_{f}Q_{f}}{gQ_{b}}=\frac{10.15(.08)+9.7(.1)}{9.81(.18)}=1009 \frac{kg}{m^3}$$

ex Streamlines
$$u=2y;\quad v=4x;\quad \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{v}{u}=\frac{2x}{y}$$
$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{2x}{y}\to ydy=2xdx\to \frac{1}{2}y^2=x^2+c\to y=\pm \sqrt{ 2x^2+c }$$
$$c=0;\quad y=\pm \sqrt{ 2x }$$
