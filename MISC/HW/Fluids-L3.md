### Rectangular Weir Measurement 1
$$h_{o}=.074m;\quad h_{w}=.122m ;\quad Q_{a}=.624 \frac{L}{s};\quad b=.03m$$
### Height above notch
$$H=h_{w}-h_{o}=.122m-.074m=.048m$$
### Expected flow rate 
$$Q_{t}=\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{ 2g }bH^{3/2}=\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{ 2\left( 9.81 \frac{m}{s^2} \right) }(.03m)(.048m)^{3/2}=.0009316 \frac{m^3}{s}$$
$$Q_{t}=.0009316 \frac{m^3}{s}\left( 1000 \frac{L}{m^3} \right)=.9316 \frac{L}{s}$$

### Measured coefficient of discharge
$$C_{d1}=\frac{Q_{a}}{Q_{t}}=\frac{.624 \frac{L}{s}}{.9316 \frac{L}{s}}=.670$$
### Expected coefficient of discharge
$$C_{d2}=.611+.075\left( \frac{H}{h_{o}} \right)=.611+.75\left( \frac{.048m}{.074m} \right)=.660$$
### Error of discharge coefficient
$$\text{Error}=\frac{\text{measured}-\text{theoretical}}{\text{theoretical}}=\frac{.67-.66}{.66}=1.52\%$$
