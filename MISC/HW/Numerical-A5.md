$$y=e^{2x};\quad y'=2e^{2x};\quad y'(0)=2$$
$$y''=4e^{2x};\quad y'''=8e^{2x};\quad y'''(0.4)=17.8043$$
$$e_{max}=\frac{h^2}{3!}f'''(\zeta)=.0296739$$
$$e_{min}=0$$
$$e=2.01335-2=.01335$$
The error is about halfway between maximum and minimum error. The error is large because the increment $h$ is small and the maximum $f'''$ value is large (17.8043).
