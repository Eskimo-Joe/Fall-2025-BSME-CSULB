### Fall Time
$$y=\frac{1}{2}gt^2\to t=\sqrt{ \frac{2y}{g} }=\sqrt{ \frac{2\left( \frac{37.75}{12}ft \right)}{32.2 \frac{ft}{s^2}} }=.442s$$
### Actual Velocity
$$v_{a}=\frac{x}{t}=\frac{\frac{101.6875}{12}ft}{.442s}=19.17 \frac{ft}{s}$$
### Expected Velocity
$$v_{t}=\sqrt{ 2gh }=\sqrt{ 2\left( 32.2 \frac{ft}{s^2} \right)\left( \frac{50.25}{12}ft \right) }=16.42 \frac{ft}{s}$$
### Coefficient of Velocity
$$C_{v}=\frac{v_{a}}{v_{t}}=\frac{19.17 \frac{ft}{s}}{16.42 \frac{ft}{s}}=1.167$$
### Vena Contracta Area
$$A_{c}=\frac{\pi}{4}D^2=\frac{\pi}{4}(.537in)^2=.226in^2$$
### Coefficient of Contraction
$$C_{c}=\frac{A_{c}}{A_{o}}=\frac{.226in^2}{.1765in^2} = 1.283$$

### Actual Coefficient of Discharge
$$C_{d_{1}}=C_{c}C_{v}=(1.283)(1.167) = 1.498$$
### Actual Volumetric Flow Rate
$$Q_{a}=\frac{\dot{m}}{\rho}=\frac{\dot{W}}{g\rho}=\frac{W}{tg\rho}=\frac{W}{t\gamma_{w}}$$
$$Q_{a}=\frac{25.6lb}{(27.7s)\left( 62.1 \frac{lb}{ft^3} \right)}=0.924 \frac{ft^3}{s}$$
### Expected Coefficient of Discharge
$$Q_{a}=C_{d_{2}}A_{o}\sqrt{ 2gh }\to C_{d_{2}}=\frac{Q_{a}}{A_{o}\sqrt{ 2gh }}$$
$$C_{d_{2}}=\frac{0.924 \frac{ft^3}{s} }{(.00122ft^2)\sqrt{ 2\left( 32.2 \frac{ft}{s^2} \right)\left( \frac{50.25}{12} ft\right) }}=0.738$$
### Kinetic Energy Rate
$$\frac{KE}{\Delta t}=\rho ghQ=\gamma hQ$$
$$\frac{KE}{\Delta t}=\left( 62.2 \frac{lb}{ft^3} \right)\left( \frac{50.25}{12} ft\right)\left( \frac{25.6lb}{27.7s}\times \frac{1 ft^3}{62.2lb} \right)=3.87 \frac{ft\cdot lb}{s}$$
