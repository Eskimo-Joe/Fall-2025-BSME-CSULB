$$T_{a}=81F=540.7R;\quad T_{b}=161F=620.7R$$
$$F=\gamma V=530lb$$
$$R_{atm}=\frac{R_{u}}{M}=\frac{49709 \frac{ftlb}{mol\cdot R}}{.001985 \frac{slug}{mol}}=25.04\times 10^6 \frac{ftlb}{\text{ slug} \cdot R}$$
$$\rho_{atm}=\frac{p}{RT}=\frac{14.9psia\left( \frac{144in^2}{1ft^2} \right)}{\left(25.04\times 10^6 \frac{ftlb}{\text{ slug} \cdot R} \right)(540.7R)}=158.46\times 10^{-9} \frac{slug}{ft^3}$$
$$\rho_{b}=\frac{14.9psia \left( \frac{144in^2}{1ft^2} \right)}{(25.04\times 10^6 \frac{ftlb}{\text{ slug} \cdot R})(620.7R)}=140.9\times 10^{-9} \text{ slug} /ft^3$$
$$\gamma_{b}=\rho_{atm}g=(158.46\times 10^{-9} \frac{slug}{ft^3})\left( 32.2 \frac{ft}{s^2} \right)=5.1\times 10^{-6} \frac{lb}{ft^3}$$
$$F_{b}=\gamma \Delta V\to \Delta V=\frac{F_{b}}{\gamma}=\frac{530lb}{5.1\times 10^{-6} \frac{lb}{ft^3}}=103.87\times 10 ^{6} ft^3$$
$$V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\to r=\left( \frac{3}{4\pi}V \right)^{1/3}=\left( \frac{3}{4\pi}103.87\times 10^6 ft^3\right)^{1/3}=291.6ft$$
