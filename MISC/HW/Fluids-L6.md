$$h_{L}=.528-.498=.030m;\quad Q=.000217 \frac{m^3}{s}$$
$$D=22mm;\quad A=\frac{\pi}{4}\left( \frac{22}{2000}m \right)^2=.00038m^2$$
$$\nu=0.000001002 \frac{m^2}{s}$$
### Flow velocity
$$Q=AV\to V=\frac{Q}{A}=\frac{.000217 \frac{m^3}{s}}{.00038m^2}=0.571 \frac{m}{s}$$
### Dynamic Head
$$h_{\text{Dynamic}}=\frac{V^2}{2g}=\frac{\left( .571 \frac{m}{s} \right)^2}{2\left( 9.81 \frac{m}{s^2} \right)}=.0166m$$
