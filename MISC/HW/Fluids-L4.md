Calculations:

### Flow, area, and static head using duct 1

$$Q=.595\frac{L}{s}=.000595 \frac{\text{ m}^3}{s} $$
$$A=5.31\times 10^{-4}\text{ m}^2;\quad h_{static}=.388\text{ m} $$

### Average flow velocity
$$Q=Av\to v=\frac{Q}{A}=\frac{.000595\frac{\text{ m} ^3}{s}}{.000531\text{ m} ^2}=1.12 \frac{\text{ m}}{s}$$

### Velocity head / Dynamic head
$$h_{dynamic}=\frac{v^2}{2g}=\frac{\left( 1.12 \frac{\text{ m}}{s} \right)^2}{2\left( 9.81 \frac{\text{ m}}{s^2}  \right)}=.064\text{ m} $$

### Total energy
$$H=h_{static}+h_{dynamic}=.452\text{ m} $$
