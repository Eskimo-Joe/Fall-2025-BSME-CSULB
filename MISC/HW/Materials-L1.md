$$T(\text{in}\cdot \text{lb});\quad \phi(\text{degrees})$$
$$T=m\phi \to 180\text{in}\cdot \text{lb}=m(5 ^{\circ})\to m=36\frac{\text{in}\cdot \text{lb}}{degree }$$
$$\tau=\frac{Tc}{J};\quad \gamma=\frac{c\phi}{L};\quad \tau=G\gamma$$
$$\frac{Tc}{J}=G \frac{c\phi}{L}\to T=\frac{JG}{L}\phi;\quad m=\frac{JG}{L}$$

## Data Point 4: 
$$T=58\text{in}\cdot \text{lb};\quad \phi=1.5^{\circ} $$
$$\phi=1.5^{\circ} =1.5\left( \frac{\pi}{180} \right)\text{ radians} =.02618\text{ radians} $$
$$L=7.375\text{in};\quad D=12.5\text{ mm} ;\quad c=6.25\text{ mm} =0.246\text{in}$$
$$J=\frac{\pi c^4}{2}=\frac{\pi(.246\text{in})^4}{2}=.00575\text{in}^4$$

#### Shear Strain
$$\gamma=\frac{c\phi}{L}=\frac{(.246\text{in})(.02618\text{ radians} )}{7.375\text{in}}=.000873\text{ radians} $$

#### Shear Stress
$$\tau=\frac{Tc}{J}=\frac{(58\text{in}\cdot \text{lb})(.246\text{in})}{.00575\text{in}^4}=2481.4\text{ psi} $$

#### Shear Modulus of Elasticity
$$G=\frac{\tau}{\gamma}=\frac{2481.4\text{psi}}{.000873 \text{ radians} }\cdot \left( \frac{1\text{ ksi} }{1000\text{psi}} \right)=2841.5\text{ ksi}$$
