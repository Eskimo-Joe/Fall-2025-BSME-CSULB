big equations
$$y=y_{0}+v_{0}t+\frac{a}{2}t^2$$
$$v=v_{0}+at$$
$a=$ constant

ball falling 11.28m 
$$y_{0}=0m;\quad t_{0}=0;\quad v_{0}=0;\quad a_{0}=g=9.8 \frac{\text{ m} }{s}$$
"concentrated rectilinear motion"
$$y=11.28m;\quad t=?;\quad v=?;\quad a=9.8 \frac{\text{ m} }{s}$$
$$y=11.28\text{ m} =0+0+\frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{9.8m}{s} \right)t^2$$
$$t=\sqrt{ \frac{2(11.28m)}{9.8 \frac{m}{s}} }= 1.517s$$
$$v=v_{0}+at=\left( 9.8 \frac{m}{s} \right)(1.517s)=14.869 \text{ m} $$

ball starts at 20m and thrown upwards 10m/s
$$y_{0}=20\text{ m} ;\quad v_{0}=\frac{10m}{s}$$
$$v=10 \frac{m}{s}-9.8 \frac{m}{s^2}t$$
integrate
$$y=\int 10-9.8t,\,dt $$
$$y=10t-\frac{9.8}{2}t^2+C$$
plug in initial condition for C
$$y=20+10t-\frac{9.8}{2}t^2$$

projectile motion
particle shot 30 degrees above horizon 180 m/s at height 150m 
$$v_{0}=180\sin(30^{\circ} )=90 \frac{m}{s};\quad y_{0}=150\text{ m} ;\quad x_{0}=0\text{ m} ;\quad t_{0}=0;\quad a=-9.8 \frac{m}{s^2}$$
$$y=-\frac{9.8}{2}t^2+90t+150=0$$
$$t=\frac{-90\pm \sqrt{ 90^2+\frac{4*150*9.8}{2} }}{-9.8};\quad t_{1}=19.91s;\quad t_{2}=-1.538s$$
$$v=-9.8t+90=-9.8\cdot 19.91+90=-105.12 \frac{m}{s}$$
$$h=413.2\text{ m} ;\quad t=9.1743s$$
