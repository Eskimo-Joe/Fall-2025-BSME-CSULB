## 10-30

$$\frac{d}{dt}(L)=\frac{d}{dt}(mv)=ma=F$$
$$\frac{d}{dt}(H)=\frac{d}{dt}r\times mv=r\times ma=M$$
$$\vec{H}=r\times \Delta mv=r\times \Delta mw\times r$$
$$\vec{H}=\omega \sum r^2\Delta m=I \vec{\omega}$$
$$\dot{H}=M=I \dot{\omega}=I\vec{\alpha} $$
