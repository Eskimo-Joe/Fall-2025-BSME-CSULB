Collisions 13.4

the line from CG to CG in collisions is line of impact
direct impact means velocity is parallel to line of impact
oblique impact has angles between velocity and line of impact

there is elastic deformation at collision

coefficient of restitution
$$e=\frac{v_{b}'-v_{a}'}{v_{a}-v_{b}}$$
if $e=0$, $v_{b}'-v_{a}'=0\to v_{a}'=v_{b}'$ then perfectly plastic impact
if $e=1$, perfectly elastic impact

ex)
A = 20kg; v = .5 m/s
B = 35kg; v = 0 m/s
After impact
vb=.3 m/s

$$m_{A}v_{A}+m_{B}v_{B}=m_{A}v_{A}'+m_{B}v_{B}'$$
$$20\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)=20v_{A}'+35(.3)\to v_{A}'=-.025 \frac{m}{s}$$
$$e=\frac{.3+.025}{.5}=.65$$


ex) 
bouncing against a wall as 30 degrees
n direction towards the wall
t parallel to the wall

$$v_{n}=v\cos{30^{\circ}};\quad v_{t}=v\sin{30^{\circ}};\quad e=0.9$$
$v_{t}'=v_{t}$ because no collision occurs in that direction

$$e=0.9=\frac{v_{wall}'-v_{n}'}{v_{n}-v_{wall}}\to -\frac{v_{n}'}{v_{n}}$$
$$v_{n}'=-0.9v_{n}=-0.779v$$
$$v'=\sqrt{ v_{n}'^2+v_{t}^2 }=\sqrt{ (-0.779v)^2+(.5v)^2 }=0.926v$$
$$\theta=tan^{-1}{\frac{v_{n}'}{v_{t}}}=32.7^{\circ}$$
if $e=1$ then $v_{n}'=-v_{n}$


ex)
two balls collide
vA=30 fps @ 30deg from LOI
vB= 40fps @ 60deg from LOI
$$e=0.9$$
$$A_{n}=30\cos{30^{\circ}};\quad A_{t}=A_{t}'=30\sin{30^{\circ}}=15$$
$$B_{n}=-40\cos{60^\circ}=-20;\quad B_{t}=B_{t}'=40\sin{60^\circ}$$
$$A_{n}+B_{n}=A_{n}'+B_{n}'$$
$$A_{n}'+B_{n}'=5.98$$
$$e=0.9=\frac{A_{n}'-B_{n}'}{B_{n}-A_{n}}$$
$$A_{n}'-B_{n}'=0.9(-20-30\cos{30^\circ})=-41.382$$
$$5.98=2B_{n}'-41.382$$
$$A_{n}'=-17.7 fps;\quad B_{n}'=23.68fps$$
trig to find resultant and angle...
$$A'=\sqrt{ A_{n}'^2+A_{t}'^2 };\quad \theta=tan^{-1}{\frac{A_{n}'}{A_{t}'}}$$
$$B'=\sqrt{ B_{n}'^2+B_{t}'^2 };\quad \theta=tan^{-1}{\frac{B_{n}'}{B_{t}'}}$$
