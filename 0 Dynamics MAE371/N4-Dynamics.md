$$\frac{d \hat{r}}{dt}=\hat{v}=\dot{x} \hat{i}+y \hat{j}+z \hat{k}$$

$$\hat{v}=v \hat{e}_{t}$$
$v$ - speed, directionless

$$\vec{a}=\dot{v} \hat{e_{t}}+v[\frac{d\hat{e}}{d\theta}+\frac{d\theta}{ds}\frac{ds}{dt}]$$
$$\frac{d\theta}{ds}=\frac{1}{r}$$
