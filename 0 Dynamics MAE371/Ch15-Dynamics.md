angular velocity - $\omega$
a branch rotates with a twig with length $r$ at angle $\phi$ from the shaft 
twig is rotated with angular velocity $\omega$
velocity of point of twig
$$v=\lim_{ t \to 0 } \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}=r\sin \phi \frac{\Delta \theta}{\Delta t}=r\dot{\theta} \sin \phi=r \omega\sin \phi$$
$$\vec{v}=\omega \times r\ne r\times \omega$$

$$\vec{a}= \frac{d}{dt} \vec{v}= \dot{\omega}\times r+ \omega \times \dot{r}$$
$$\vec{a}=\alpha \times r+\omega \times (\omega \times r)$$
$$a=\alpha r$$
